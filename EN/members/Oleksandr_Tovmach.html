Subsoil Capillary Irrigation System for Plants

A subsoil capillary irrigation system for agriculture, gardening, and parks. Includes moisture dispensers and rupture detectors. Enables uniform, timely, and economical irrigation of plants by placing the irrigation system underground and supplying water of the required temperature through soil capillaries.

Table of product/service features, advantages, and benefits

Features Advantages Benefits
Placement of irrigation system under the soil Allows watering with water of the necessary temperature without harming plants
Prevents soil erosion 		For corn:
- prevents degradation of 0.5 ha of soil (an additional 3 tons of corn yield)
- saves up to €40 per hectare per watering
- increases crop yield by 10% or more
Presence of moisture dispenser Saves at least 50% water, enables targeted application of mineral fertilizers, and convenient use of organic bioproducts (like "Kyussey", prof. Higa Teru) Can be used in combination with other agrotechnologies for various conditions (in climate chambers, hilly terrain, etc.)
System damage/breakage detector Timely diagnostics and repairs, thus preventing soil drying Profit for conditions in Ukraine: no less than €460,000 per hectare
  Modularity  

The system includes a technical and economic assessment per hectare for conditions in Ukraine. The project was developed within the “Commercialization Pathfinder” training by CRDF Global & IC2 Institute, University of Texas at Austin (project manager: Yuliya Shutyak).

There is a technical and economic assessment for irrigation of one hectare. Additional research is needed to develop a prototype.

Proposals for Localizing Household Sewage Pollution

Proposals regarding toilets, dishwashing, and laundry: how these processes can avoid polluting the environment. Let's start with something simple.

The idea is to develop a small family business – a “laundry”. Concentrating washing agents in these mini-firms would allow full purification of wastewater, removing environmentally hazardous substances.
